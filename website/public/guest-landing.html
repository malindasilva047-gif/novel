Questions, answered

Can I earn from my stories?

We are building ways for authors to earn from their work. They are not available yet, so for now the best thing you can do is keep writing and grow your readers. Everything you publish now will be waiting for them.

Is it free to read?

Yes. Most stories are free. Some authors offer paid chapters or early access.

How do I start publishing?

Create an account, open the editor and write your first chapter. You can save it as a draft until you are ready.

Can I read offline?

Yes, you can read smoothly directly through our web platform on any device.

Is there a mobile app?

Not right now. For now the website works seamlessly on any phone or desktop browser.
